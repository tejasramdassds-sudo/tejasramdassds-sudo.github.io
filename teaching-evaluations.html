I also noticed that any time I would email him with a question about a topic or administrative details of the course, his average response time to my emails (some of which were sent well outside of standard business hours) was quite literally three minutes.

Evaluation questions and scale

  1. Was the recitation organized and clear?

    1 = Disorganized and unclear; 5 = Very organized and lucid.

  2. Was the teaching assistant willing and available to help you overcome difficulties?

    1 = Was of no help; 5 = Was very helpful.

  3. How would you rate your teaching assistant's command of the course material?

    1 = Poor command of material; 5 = Excellent command of material.

  4. What was the overall quality of your interaction with the teaching assistant?

    1 = Low, taught me little; 5 = High, extremely educational.

Written prompts: “Please comment on any aspect of this recitation.” and “Would you recommend this TA to someone considering taking a course assisted by him or her?”
